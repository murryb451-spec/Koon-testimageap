Koon Sets Out To Explore

How To Play ?

🐾 Step 1: Select any of the 4 locations on the map grid to send Koon exploring.

☕ Step 2: Tap an action choice inside that area to search for hidden coffee collectibles.

💎 Step 3: Build your mastery percentage! Collect Common (80%), Rare (18%), and Legendary (2%) item sets to load up your backpack!
